Zetacoin and ZetaChain are separate projects that share a similar name and nothing else. Zetacoin trades as ZET on its own Scrypt chain with `9...` addresses, launched in 2013, and is held in Zetacoin Wallet. ZetaChain is a different network with a different token and no connection to Zetacoin's chain, supply or software.

## The difference in one table

| Question | Zetacoin | ZetaChain |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Ticker | ZET | a different ticker, not ZET |
| Launched | 3 August 2013 | a later project with its own launch |
| Chain | its own chain, Scrypt, hybrid PoW and PoS | its own separate network |
| Block time | 30 seconds | set by that project |
| Address format | `9...` on the current chain | unrelated format |
| Supply | approximately 59.9 million ZET | set by that project |
| Software | Zetacoin Wallet, open source desktop client | that project's own tooling |
| Maintainer | EWMCI, LLC | that project's team |

Nothing in the left column is shared with the right column. A wallet, exchange listing or campaign that treats the two as interchangeable is describing neither accurately.

The full walk-through, with the checks in order, is in [What Is Zetacoin? The Scrypt Coin Behind the ZET Wallet](https://github.com/zetacoin-wallet/zetacoin-wallet-guides/blob/main/what-is-the-zet-coin.md).

## Why the confusion happens

The names collide at the front, the tickers look alike at a glance, and search engines have never fully separated them. A search for the Zetacoin wallet can surface ZetaChain wallets, and a search for ZetaChain can surface pages about ZET, because both strings share the same stem.

The result is visible in what people ask: whether the coins are related, whether a ZETA purchase gives exposure to Zetacoin, and whether an airdrop for one applies to the other. All three have the same answer  -  no  -  but the question keeps being asked because the naming keeps generating it.

There is a second layer to the confusion. Both names appear in crypto news cycles at different times, so a reader who encounters one headline and one wallet listing can reasonably assume a single project with a rebrand. That assumption is wrong here: Zetacoin's chain has run since 2013 and its rebuild in 2021 kept the same coin and the same community, while the other project is a separate network that never held ZET.

A third layer is technical vocabulary. Zetacoin's consensus is hybrid Proof-of-Work and Proof-of-Stake with 30-second blocks and emission under 50,000 ZET a year, which is a very different design from a network built around cross-chain messaging. When a comparison article lists features from both under one heading, the feature list is the give-away: staking rewards of 0.0625 ZET and a `9...` address format can only describe Zetacoin, and anything about routing assets between chains cannot.

## How to tell which one you are looking at

Use mechanical checks rather than descriptions. Ticker, address prefix, chain, and the source of the software. Any one of them is enough to separate the two, and all four together leave no ambiguity.

1. Read the ticker on the venue or wallet. ZET is Zetacoin; anything else is not.
2. Look at an address. `9...` belongs to Zetacoin's current chain, `Z...` to its retired chain, and neither belongs to another network.
3. Check the software's origin. Zetacoin Wallet comes from the project's own release page and nowhere else.
4. Check the block explorer. A height above 4.6 million with 30-second blocks is Zetacoin's chain.

If a page fails two or more of these, it is not about this coin regardless of what it says at the top.

For the background behind this step, [What the Zetacoin Desktop Client Does](https://github.com/zetacoin-wallet/zetacoin-wallet-guides/blob/main/what-the-desktop-client-does.md) covers the same ground in more detail.

## What to do if you bought the wrong one

If you bought the other project's token expecting Zetacoin, the purchase is a transaction on that other network and Zetacoin's team cannot reverse it. There is no conversion between the two, no swap, and no redemption  -  the assets are unrelated.

What you can do is stop before adding to the position and check what you actually hold. Read the ticker on the exchange, withdraw a small test amount to a wallet you control, and confirm the address prefix. If the address does not begin with `9`, it is not a Zetacoin address.

This matters because the wrong-asset mistake compounds: people who believe they hold ZET sometimes send the other token to a Zetacoin address, or expect Zetacoin staking rewards from a balance that is not on Zetacoin's chain. Neither works, and neither is recoverable by the project.

## Where each name legitimately appears

Zetacoin appears on its own site, its GitHub repository and release page, its block explorer, its community channels, and the smaller exchanges that list ZET. Those are the places where the name means this coin.

Elsewhere the name is often a keyword rather than a subject: an aggregator page templated across thousands of coins, a wallet comparison that lists both, a forum thread from years ago. None of those are authoritative, and some of them are actively misleading about status or ticker.

The rule that follows is simple: verify against the project's own release page and chain, and treat every other mention as a claim to check rather than a fact to act on. {{ARTICLE:where-to-download-official-zetacoin-wallet}} exists precisely because lookalike names and domains are the practical risk here.

## Common questions

### Is Zetacoin the same as ZetaChain?

No. They are separate projects with separate chains, tokens, supplies and software. Zetacoin launched in 2013, trades as ZET and uses `9...` addresses; the other project has its own network and token.

### What is ZetaChain crypto?

It is a different blockchain project with its own token and ecosystem, unrelated to Zetacoin's chain, supply or wallet. Nothing about its token applies to ZET.

### I bought ZETA thinking it was Zetacoin. Are they related?

No, and the purchase cannot be converted. Check the ticker and address prefix on what you hold; Zetacoin addresses begin with `9`, and only ZET balances stake or transfer on Zetacoin's chain.

### Can I swap one for the other?

No. There is no bridge, swap or redemption between the two projects, and any service offering one is not describing a real mechanism.
